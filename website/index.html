Section 02 — The Workshop

Built slowly,
by the same hands
for seventeen years.

Every Timber Home piece begins as a rough plank in our atelier outside Prishtinë. We mill, joinery-cut and finish in-house — no outsourcing, no shortcuts, no veneer pretending to be solid.

"A door should outlive the house it opens into."

Our timber is sourced from FSC-certified Balkan forests within 200km of the workshop. Brass hardware is hand-cast in Gjakova. Linen oil finishes are pressed within the same valley.
